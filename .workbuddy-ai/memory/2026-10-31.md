## Footer wordmark — full name in halftone

- `HalftoneWordmarkFooter` now supports a **stacked wordmark** (split `wordmark` on `\n`, one
  shared size set by the widest line, centred pyramid) plus two new props: `bottomGap`
  (clear space under the block) and `fitWidth` (fraction of width the widest line fills).
- Footer passes `SHREE / KESHRI / SYNTHETICS`, `crop=0`, `bottomGap=60`, `fitWidth=0.84`,
  `dotGap=5`, `fontWeight=800` (Inter 800 added to the font link), height `clamp(540,46vw,660)`.
- On ≤700px the dot gap drops to 3.1 and the word is fitted to 0.94 with a taller box, otherwise
  the halftone turns to mush at 390px.
- A new dark "A note on the shop" section follows the halftone block with the closing paragraph
  and the back-to-top link (moved out of the component via `backToTop={false}`).
